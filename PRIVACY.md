# Tidy Tabs privacy policy

Tidy Tabs does not collect, send or sell any data.

- **No network access.** The extension makes no network requests: no analytics, no tracking, no remote code, no server.
- **Tab information stays in your browser.** Tidy Tabs reads the titles and addresses of your open tabs only to count them, search them, close or move them, and group them, as you set it up. This information is never stored outside your browser and never leaves your device.
- **Settings.** Your settings (tab limit, groups, ignored sites) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them across your own devices through your Google account, like any other Chrome setting. Tidy Tabs has no access to that copy.
- **Permissions.** `tabs` and `tabGroups` let the extension count, search, close, move and group tabs. `storage` saves your settings. `notifications` tells you when the tab limit blocks or closes a tab. Tidy Tabs requests no access to the content of web pages.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/o0Zz/tidy-tabs/issues.
